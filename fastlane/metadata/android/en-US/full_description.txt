When did you last water the plants? Change the bedsheets? Call your grandmother?

Time Clicker tells you at a glance. Each tile shows how long ago you last did something, ticking live: "37 seconds", "3 days 5 hours", "1 year 12 days". When you do it again, tap the tile and it starts over.

ONE TAP
• Tap a tile to mark it as done now
• Tapped by mistake? Tap again within 10 seconds to undo
• A counter shows how many times each tile was pressed

REMINDERS
Get a notification when something hasn't been done for a while: after so many hours, days or weeks. Mark it as done right from the notification.

GROUPS
Sort your tiles into groups, like Home, Health or Pets, and swipe between them.

HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
Put any tile on your home screen. Tap the widget to mark it as done without opening the app.

MAKE IT YOURS
• 12 soft colours, or one of your photos as the background
• About 1,900 icons, with search
• Three tile sizes: small, wide and tall
• Light and dark themes, and Material You colours on Android 12 and later
• Made for phones and tablets, in portrait, landscape and split screen

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. The app doesn't even have internet access: everything stays on your device. Export all your tiles, history and photos to a single file, and import it on your next phone.

Ideas to track: watering the plants, changing the sheets, a haircut, calling a friend, cleaning the fridge, the car's oil change, the cat's flea treatment, a new toothbrush, descaling the coffee machine…

Available in 16 languages.
