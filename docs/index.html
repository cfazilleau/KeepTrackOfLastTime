Time Clicker

Tracks when you last did something. Each tile shows how long ago, ticking live. Tap it when you do it again.

Or download the APK from GitHub.

Features

  • UndoTap again within 10 seconds to undo a press.
  • GroupsSort tiles into groups and swipe between them.
  • RemindersGet notified when something hasn't been done for a while.
  • WidgetsPut any tile on your home screen and tap it there.
  • Make it yoursColours, photos, about 1,900 icons, three tile sizes.
  • Material YouLight and dark themes, following your system colours on Android 12+.
  • 16 languagesFrom English and French to Japanese and Arabic.
  • Fully offlineNo account, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone; export it as one file.
